# The World's Most Beautiful Running Routes You Should Plan a Trip Around

For many runners, a great route is reason enough to book a flight. **The world's most beautiful running routes you should plan a trip around** span every terrain imaginable, from cliff-hugging coastal paths in Madeira and the Amalfi Coast to volcanic trails on Réunion Island, alpine traverses in Washington State, and marathon courses that wind past the Great Wall of China or along California's Highway 1. These are not just workouts. They are full travel experiences built around the rhythm of your stride.

![Runners on a scenic coastal trail with ocean cliffs and blue water in the background](/blog/assets/the-worlds-most-beautiful-running-routes-you-should-plan-a-trip-around/image_1.jpg)

What separates a truly memorable running route from an ordinary one is the sense that the landscape is revealing itself to you in a way no car window ever could. Whether you are chasing dramatic scenery, cultural immersion, or a personal challenge, the routes covered here offer something worth traveling for.

## Why Runners Are Planning Entire Trips Around a Single Route

The "runcation" has become one of travel's fastest-growing trends. Runners are no longer choosing a destination and then finding a route to fill a morning. They are choosing the route first and building the entire trip around it. A dramatic coastal path or a high-altitude traverse becomes the anchor, with flights, accommodation, and rest days arranged to support it.

Scenic motivation is real. When the landscape is extraordinary, effort feels lighter. There is also something deeply satisfying about earning a view through physical effort rather than a cable car ticket. Many of the most beautiful runs in the world require no entry fee, no bib number, and no start time. They are simply there, waiting.

## How to Choose the Right Scenic Running Route for Your Trip

Matching a route to your fitness level and travel style prevents disappointment. Consider these factors before booking:

- **Terrain type**: coastal paths suit road runners transitioning to trails; alpine routes demand technical footing and elevation experience
- **Distance and elevation**: a 7km coastal walk-run differs enormously from a 93-mile alpine loop
- **Season and weather**: many high-altitude routes are only accessible between June and September; coastal routes often shine in shoulder seasons
- **Race entry vs free trail**: some routes require race registration months in advance; others are open year-round with no permit needed

## Coastal Classics Worth Planning a Trip Around

### Bondi to Coogee, Sydney, Australia

This 7km clifftop path is one of the world's all-time classic short runs, passing ancient Aboriginal rock carvings, the Bronte Ocean Pool, and a series of dramatic coves. Best run in the cooler months of May through September.

### Big Sur International Marathon, California, USA

Ranked the most beautiful marathon in the world by a SportsShoes.com eye-tracking study with a score of 99 out of 100, Big Sur follows Highway 1 past Bixby Bridge, redwood forest, and Pacific cliffs. Entry costs approximately $295 and the field is limited to around 4,500 runners.

### Two Oceans Marathon, Cape Town, South Africa

This 56km ultra traces two ocean coastlines, climbing Chapman's Peak Drive above turquoise waves before finishing at the University of Cape Town. Early autumn conditions bring cool mornings and exceptional community atmosphere.

### Vereda do Larano, Madeira, Portugal

This 9-mile trail links Porto da Cruz to Machico along Atlantic cliffs and through ancient laurel forest. [National Geographic's scenic route guide](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/travel/article/worlds-best-scenic-runs-worth-travelling-for) highlights it as one of Europe's most inspiring runs.

## Iconic Trail Runs Worth a Transatlantic Flight

![Runner navigating a high-altitude mountain trail with snow-capped peaks in the background](/blog/assets/the-worlds-most-beautiful-running-routes-you-should-plan-a-trip-around/image_2.jpg)

### Path of the Gods, Amalfi Coast, Italy

The Sentiero degli Dei clings to the cliffside above the Tyrrhenian Sea, connecting villages with sweeping Mediterranean panoramas, stone steps, terraced lemon groves, and the scent of wild herbs underfoot.

### GR20, Corsica, France

Covering roughly 112 miles along Corsica's granite spine, the GR20 is widely considered Europe's most demanding long-distance trail. Most runners tackle it as a multi-day fastpack over a week or more.

### Wonderland Trail, Mount Rainier, USA

Circling Mount Rainier over 93 miles with approximately 25,000 feet of total elevation change, the Wonderland Trail passes through alpine meadows and old-growth forest. A permit is required, and the full loop is one of North America's most demanding running challenges.

## Heritage and Landmark Races

### Great Wall Marathon, China

With 5,164 steps incorporated into the course, this UNESCO-listed race in Tianjin province is less a run and more an ascent through history. The SportsShoes.com study ranked it second most beautiful in the world.

### Athens Classic Marathon, Greece

Following the original route from Marathon to Athens, this race finishes inside the Panathenaic Stadium, the site of the first modern Olympics. Every footstep carries historical resonance.

### Loch Ness Marathon, Scotland

Ranked third most beautiful marathon in the world, this Highland route follows Loch Ness into Inverness with autumnal colour, a pipe band start, and the ever-present possibility of spotting Nessie.

## Wild and Remote Routes for the Adventurous Runner

### Réunion Island Trail Network

With over 600 miles of trails crossing volcanic terrain, Réunion Island is a trail runner's paradise. A 9-mile run on Piton de la Fournaise crosses active lava fields with dramatic views.

### Pouākai Circuit, New Zealand

This demanding day loop around Mount Taranaki involves significant climbing and delivers dramatic alpine views across rugged forest and remote wilderness.

### Haleakalā National Park, Maui, Hawaii

Running inside the crater of a dormant volcano at altitude is a genuinely surreal experience. A permit system controls access, and timing a run to coincide with sunrise transforms the landscape into something otherworldly.

## Practical Tips for Planning Your Running Route Trip

![Runner preparing gear and checking a trail map before a scenic destination run](/blog/assets/the-worlds-most-beautiful-running-routes-you-should-plan-a-trip-around/image_3.jpg)

Good planning turns a great route into a great trip. Keep these points in mind:

- **Book race entries early**: popular events like Big Sur and Two Oceans sell out months in advance
- **Gear checklist**: trail shoes for technical terrain, road shoes for paved heritage courses, and a hydration vest for anything over 90 minutes
- **Accommodation**: staying near the trailhead saves time and reduces pre-run stress
- **Nutrition and hydration**: heat and altitude both increase fluid needs significantly; research aid stations or carry your own
- **Training preparation**: build progressively over 12 to 20 weeks, include terrain-specific training, and align your training peak with your travel date

The world's most beautiful running routes reward those who plan well and train specifically. Choose your terrain, build your fitness, and let the route justify the journey.
