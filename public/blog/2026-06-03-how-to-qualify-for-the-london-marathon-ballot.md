# How to Qualify for the London Marathon Ballot

Understanding **how to qualify for the London Marathon ballot** is the first step toward toeing the start line of one of the world's most iconic races. The public ballot is open to anyone aged 18 or over, requires no prior marathon experience, and is completely free to enter. However, competition is fierce - over a million people applied for the 2025 race, with only around 17,000 ballot places available, giving entrants roughly a 2% chance of success.

![Runners crossing the finish line at the London Marathon with crowds cheering along the course](/blog/assets/how-to-qualify-for-the-london-marathon-ballot/image_1.jpg)

The ballot is a random draw, with results announced by email in early July. Beyond the ballot, there are several other routes into the race, including Good For Age qualification, Championship entry, charity places, and official tour operators. This guide covers every path so you can find the best option for your situation.

## What Makes the London Marathon So Hard to Enter?

The London Marathon holds the record for the world's largest marathon ballot. The 2025 ballot attracted over 1.1 million applicants, with only around 17,000 to 20,000 public ballot places available each year, putting your odds at roughly 2%.

First held in 1981, the race is one of the six Abbott World Marathon Majors alongside Boston, Berlin, Chicago, New York, and Tokyo. The 2025 race saw 56,640 finishers. That combination of history, prestige, and scale makes demand almost impossible to satisfy through the ballot alone.

## How to Qualify for the London Marathon Ballot: Step-by-Step

The ballot is open to anyone aged 18 or over on race day. Here is how the process works:

- Visit tcslondonmarathon.com during the entry window, which typically opens in late April and closes in early May
- Complete your entry form; you will receive a confirmation email shortly after
- The draw is completely random, similar to the National Lottery
- Results are announced by email in early July; check your junk folder if nothing arrives

### How the "Double Your Chances" Option Works

When entering, you can select the "Double Your Chances" option:

- Your entry fee drops from £79.99 to £49.99, paid upfront as a donation
- If you miss out in the main draw, you are automatically entered into a second ballot
- If you miss out in both draws, you receive a free hydration vest worth over £100

### Entry Fees

| Entry Type | Fee | When You Pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UK standard ballot | £79.99 | Only if successful |
| UK donation / Double Your Chances | £49.99 | Upfront at entry |
| International entrants | £225 (includes carbon offset) | Only if successful |

## Other Ways to Get Into the London Marathon

The ballot is not the only route. Several structured pathways exist, and pursuing more than one simultaneously is a sensible strategy.

### Good For Age (GFA) Entry

Good For Age entry is available to UK residents who have run a qualifying marathon time within the designated period. For the 2026 race, the qualifying window ran from 1 October 2024 to 30 September 2025. 6,000 GFA places are available, allocated on a fastest-first basis.

| Age Group | Women | Men |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 18-39 | sub 3:38 | sub 2:52 |
| 40-44 | sub 3:43 | sub 2:57 |
| 45-49 | sub 3:46 | sub 3:02 |
| 50-54 | sub 3:53 | sub 3:07 |
| 55-59 | sub 3:58 | sub 3:12 |
| 60-64 | sub 4:23 | sub 3:34 |
| 65-69 | sub 4:53 | sub 3:52 |
| 70+ | Progressively adjusted | Progressively adjusted |

Your time must come from a certified in-person marathon course. If your time is within 10 minutes of the GFA standard, entering the ballot as a backup is recommended. Full details are on the [Good for Age entry page](https://www.londonmarathonevents.co.uk/london-marathon/good-age-entry).

### Championship Entry

Championship entry is reserved for the quickest club runners:

- Marathon qualifying standard: sub-2:38 for men, sub-3:10 for women
- Half marathon qualifying standard: sub-1:11:30 for men, sub-1:26:00 for women
- 1,200 places available, allocated fastest-first
- You must hold a current UK athletics membership
- Falsification of times results in a ban of up to five years

### Charity and Club Places

Around 500 partner charities hold approximately 25,000 bibs each year, making charity entry one of the most reliable alternative routes. Fundraising minimums are typically £1,500 or more. British Athletics affiliated clubs also receive guaranteed places based on club size, and international runners can access the race through official overseas tour operators listed on the London Marathon website.

## How to Improve Your Chances in the London Marathon Ballot

No single tactic guarantees success, but combining several approaches meaningfully improves your odds:

- Enter every year; cumulative attempts increase your lifetime probability of success
- Always select the Double Your Chances option to access the second ballot draw at a lower fee
- Pursue multiple routes simultaneously, such as entering the ballot while also applying for GFA or a charity place
- Check your email and junk folder in early July so you do not miss a successful notification

## Frequently Asked Questions About the London Marathon Ballot

### How to Qualify for the London Marathon Ballot if You Live Outside the UK?

International runners have a separate entry path:

- Enter via the international ballot on the London Marathon website
- Apply through an official overseas tour operator for a combined travel and entry package
- Championship entry is available to international runners who hold a qualifying UK athletics membership

### When Does the London Marathon Ballot Open and Close?

- The ballot opens in late April each year and closes in early May
- Results are emailed in early July
- [Enter the ballot](https://www.londonmarathonevents.co.uk/ballot) via the official London Marathon Events site

### What Happens If You Don't Get a Ballot Place?

Missing out in the ballot does not end your options:

- If you selected Double Your Chances, you are automatically entered into the second ballot
- Charity entry remains open after the ballot closes
- The GFA application window typically opens in autumn, giving you time to qualify
- Re-enter the ballot the following year; it resets annually and each entry is a fresh draw
