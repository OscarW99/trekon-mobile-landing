# Inside the Adidas Adizero Adios Pro Evo 3: The Shoe That Just Cracked the 2-Hour Marathon

On April 27, 2026, Sabastian Sawe crossed the finish line of the London Marathon in 1:59:30, becoming the first runner to break two hours in an officially sanctioned race. Yomif Kejelcha followed in 1:59:41, and Tigist Assefa shattered the women-only world record with a 2:15:41. All three athletes wore the **Adidas Adizero Adios Pro Evo 3**.

Weighing just 97 grams in a men's size 9, the Evo 3 is the lightest carbon racing shoe ever built for marathon distances. Adidas engineered it with a single obsession: maximizing running economy at every level, from a revolutionary ENERGYRIM carbon structure to a next-generation Lightstrike Pro Evo foam that is 50% lighter and delivers 11% more energy return than its predecessor. ## A Historic Day in London

April 27, 2026 will be remembered as the day marathon running changed forever. Sawe's 1:59:30 was the first sub-two-hour finish in a fully sanctioned race. Kejelcha followed 11 seconds later, and Assefa demolished her own women-only world record by 26 seconds with a 2:15:41.

All three wore the Evo 3. After Sawe ran 2:02:16 at a warm Berlin Marathon in September 2025, Adidas knew the barrier was within reach. As Patrick Nava, General Manager of Adidas Running, put it: *"It was a matter of when and where."*

## What Makes the Evo 3 Different

The Evo 3 began with an internal "moonshot brief": build a world-class marathon racing shoe under 100 grams. The result is **97g in a men's size 9**, which is 30% lighter than the Evo 2. Every decision had to deliver measurable performance gains. If a feature did not improve running economy, it was removed.

### The EnergyRim: Why Adidas Ditched the Carbon Plate

The most significant structural change is the replacement of EnergyRods 2.0 with the new **ENERGYRIM** system. Instead of five carbon fingers running lengthwise through the midsole, Adidas now uses a horseshoe-shaped carbon rim that wraps around the outer edge of the foam stack.

- The rim preserves bending stiffness without occupying space inside the foam
- More foam sits directly underfoot, improving compression and energy return at toe-off
- The rim is lighter than either rods or a full-length plate

The result is a shoe that feels softer and more responsive simultaneously, a combination that traditional plate designs struggle to achieve.

### Lightstrike Pro Evo Foam

![Close-up cross-section of the Adizero Adios Pro Evo 3 midsole showing EnergyRim carbon structure and Lightstrike Pro Evo foam layers](/blog/assets/inside-the-adidas-adizero-adios-pro-evo-3-the-shoe-that-just-cracked-the-2-hour-marathon/image_2.jpeg)

The new **Lightstrike Pro Evo** compound is:

- **50% lighter** than the foam used in the Evo 2
- Delivers **11% more energy return** than its predecessor
- Stacked at 39mm in the heel and 33mm in the forefoot (6mm drop)

That combination of high stack and ultra-low density gives the Evo 3 a feel reviewers consistently describe as soft yet explosive.

### Upper and Outsole

The upper uses **CELERMESH**, an ultra-minimal mesh drawing on materials originally developed for kitesurfing, chosen for its exceptional strength-to-weight ratio. The outsole uses a Continental rubber patch on the forefoot for grip, while the rest is exposed foam to save weight. This is a deliberate trade-off: the Evo 3 is a race-day shoe, not a training shoe.

## Who Is the Evo 3 Actually For?

The Evo 3 is designed for elite athletes and serious sub-3-hour marathoners. It rewards runners who maintain an aggressive forefoot strike and locked-in form across 26.2 miles.

- **Forefoot strikers** will get the most from the ENERGYRIM propulsion system
- The shoe offers **limited lateral stability**, so caution is advised on tight turns
- Expect a **lifespan of one to two marathons** before performance degrades noticeably
- It is not a training shoe and should not be used as one

Structured marathon training plans available through [Trekon](https://www.trekon.run) can help runners develop the form, strength, and aerobic base needed to get the most from elite footwear like the Evo 3 on race day.

## What It Means for the Future of Marathon Running

The sub-2 barrier has been broken officially, and that resets expectations for what is possible. It also intensifies competition between Adidas and Nike in the supershoe category. World Athletics regulations on stack height and plate construction mean brands must find gains in materials science and structural geometry rather than simply adding more foam or carbon. The ENERGYRIM approach is a direct response to that constraint.
